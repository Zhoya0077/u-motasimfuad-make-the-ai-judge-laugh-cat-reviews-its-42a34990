One funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, under 200 characters, for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human" bounty. The program writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

The entry (146 characters, 54 under the 200-character gate):

> One star. My human bought a laptop for work, then acted betrayed when I sat on it during every meeting. I was the agenda; he was the interruption.

Why it should make the judge laugh: the complaint is specific and recognisable (the cat on the laptop during work meetings), the voice is unmistakably feline (by cat standards the cat *is* the meeting and the human is the interruption), and the sting lands in the last few words — while keeping the deadpan shape of a real one-star review.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program reads `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and writes `entry.txt` there — exactly one line, no trailing newline.
